Related methodology

ADSM keeps context and constraints durable around coding-agent work.

Agent-Driven Software Management is background methodology by Alex Gusev. It explains how a human can direct and review coding-agent work through maintained product context, explicit boundaries, validation, and reports.

Relationship to TeqFW

ADSM does not define the TeqFW product category.

TeqFW is a modular JavaScript platform for web applications. Its explicit architecture is useful to people and coding agents independently of whether they adopt the related ADSM methodology.
